Dossier.
Tools Source

Every PDF tool you actually need. None of the uploading.

Merge, split, compress, convert, watermark, sign and lock down your PDFs — processed right here in your browser tab. Your files never touch a server.

● No uploads. No accounts. No file size limits from a server you don't control.

Organize

Merge PDFs

Combine several PDFs into one, in any order you choose.

Split a PDF

Pull out a page range, or break every page into its own file.

Rotate pages

Fix sideways or upside-down pages, one by one or all at once.
Convert

PDF to JPG

Export every page as a high-resolution JPG image.

JPG to PDF

Turn a batch of photos or scans into one tidy PDF.

Compress a PDF

Shrink file size by re-encoding pages at a quality you pick.
Customize & secure

Add a watermark

Stamp text across every page — drafts, confidential, your logo text.

Add text to a PDF

Drop text boxes anywhere on the page — fill a form, sign a line.

Password protect

Lock a PDF with a password so only your recipient can open it.
Dossier runs entirely as static files — every conversion happens on your device with open-source libraries (pdf-lib, pdf.js). Fork it, host it yourself, read every line.
