Compress a PDF

Dossier re-renders each page as a JPEG at a quality you choose and rebuilds the PDF around it. Works best on scans and image-heavy PDFs; text-only PDFs are already small and won't shrink much this way.

Drop a PDF here, or click to browse
One file at a time.
    How this works: each page is drawn to a canvas by pdf.js, re-encoded as JPEG at your chosen quality, and reassembled into a new PDF with pdf-lib — all locally.
